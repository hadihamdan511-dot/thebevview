The Vibe

Altitude changes everything

Perched above Qanat Bakish

Set in the pine-covered hills of Baskinta, The Bevview looks out over a valley that catches the last light of day like nowhere else in Lebanon. Terraced seating steps down the mountainside toward the view, so every table gets its share of the horizon.

Come for the sunset, stay for the night — cozy bungalows dot the property for those who'd rather not rush the mountain air, and the lounge stays unhurried well past dark.

Panoramic sunsets, unhurried evenings

The golden hour is treated like a scheduled event here — low lighting, warm textiles, and a menu built to move slowly from mezza to embers to dessert. Nothing about the evening feels rushed.

Whether it's a family Sunday lunch on the Lebanese formula or a late-night shisha session with the full bar list, the mountain sets the pace.
